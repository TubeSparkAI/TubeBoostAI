Growth Insights

Practical advice to help your content stand out and get discovered.

Keep It Short

Short titles are easier to read on small screens and are not cut off in search results. Aim for 25 to 40 characters.

Show Your Face

Thumbnails with a clear, expressive face build trust and help viewers recognise your channel at a glance.

Use One Hashtag

A single relevant hashtag looks clean and helps discovery without making the title feel spammy.

High Contrast Wins

Use bright text on a darker background and keep it to four words or fewer so it stays readable when small.

Be Consistent

Reuse the same colours, fonts and layout so viewers recognise your content instantly in a crowded feed.

Test and Improve

Try two title variations over time and compare click-through rate. Small improvements compound into big growth.
